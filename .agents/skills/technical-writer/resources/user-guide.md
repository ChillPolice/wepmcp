# Pulsera User Guide

Welcome to Pulsera! This guide will help you understand how to use the application.

## Interacting with the AI Assistant
Pulsera features a built-in AI Assistant designed to help you analyze trends.

### Opening the Chat
1. In the top navigation bar, locate the chat icon (speech bubble) on the right side, next to the theme dropdown.
2. Click the icon to toggle the chat panel.
3. The AI Assistant chat panel will slide in on the right side of the screen.

### Sending Messages
1. Type your message in the input field at the bottom of the chat panel. The field will automatically resize to fit your text.
2. Press the **Enter** key or click the upward arrow button (**↑**) to send your message.
3. If you need to add a new line to your message without sending it, press **Shift + Enter**.

*Note: The current AI is a mock service that counts the characters in your message to demonstrate functionality. It will be upgraded to a real LLM in future updates.*
